anitabuilder privacy

Last updated 23 October 2026

anitabuilder is a railway building game that runs in your browser. It is a hobby project run by one person, Anita. This page describes what it stores about you.

Without an account

Your games are saved in your own browser and are never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

With an account

Signing in is optional. The account is the same one used by noritetsu, another map on this site, so signing in on one signs you in on the other. If you sign in, the server keeps:

  • With Google sign-in, the account number Google gives this app. Your Google name, email address and photo are not stored.
  • With a username and password, your username and a one-way hash of your password (the password itself is not stored), plus a recovery email address if you add one.
  • For each game you play while signed in: its points in total and per city, and its game day. Sandbox games send nothing but the fact that they are sandboxes. Your games themselves stay in your browser.
  • If you join the leaderboards, the display name you choose.

A recovery email is used only to confirm the address and to send password reset links. Those emails are sent through Resend.

What other people can see

If you join the leaderboards, your display name and your best points are public, on the world leaderboard and on the leaderboard of each city where you have points. Your username and email are never shown to anyone. Nothing is public if you do not join.

Other things

There are no ads, and anitabuilder does not use tracking cookies or sell or share your data. The server sees your IP address with each request and uses it to limit abuse; it is not kept with your account. The game loads its data from Cloudflare and its map from OpenFreeMap, and the sign-in button from Google when you open the account panel; those services see requests as they would from any website.

Leaderboard entries are not currently backed up.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from the account panel in the game or on noritetsu. That removes your login details, email address, scores and leaderboard entries, and your noritetsu rides, from the server straight away. Deleting a saved game while signed in removes its scores.
