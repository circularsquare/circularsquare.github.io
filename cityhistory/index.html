About

Track city populations through history!

Clicking a city traces its history on the graph. A line is full thickness where there is a population estimate near that year, and thins where it is interpolating between distant estimates. A distant interpolated city has its bubble on the map drawn at half opacity. For very long intervals, we disappear the bubble.

The timeline is stretched out for recent times and compressed for the distant past.

Historical populations from Stadestér, which compiles Chandler, Bairoch/Buringh, de Vries, Populstat and others. Figures before 1800 are estimates and often disagree between sources. The modern era comes from UN WUP 2025.

Populstat usually measures the metro area population of a city, while WUP measures the connected urban area above a density threshold. This definition difference is responsible for a jump near the year 2000, which is drawn on the graph as a dashed line.

Basemap by OpenFreeMap from OpenStreetMap data, built with MapLibre GL.
