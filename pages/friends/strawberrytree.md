strawberry tree is a shrub in the ericaceae family that grows in dry hilly areas, like where i went to college. the flowers are clusters of small pink bells which hummingbirds love.

the fruits turn from yellow to red as they ripen. they have a nice tangy flavor.

he is vibrant. he is doing research somewhere in [al alta](/places/lakta/). he donates 60% of his income to the company he works at. he is abundant in faith. i admire it. 

he believes that he is in the center of the world. in a perfect world, we would all believe such a thing. in a natural world. it is only the tragedy of [streams](/thoughts/trancin/) that makes it otherwise, and this is the root of much sadness... the more time that passes, the more clear it becomes that he is at the top of the world too. i trust him very much, because of how often he has proved me wrong.

when i'm alone with you i feel in motion, light. briefly, your aspiration goes through me and i can use it out of my own mouth. i wish i could see you more often...
