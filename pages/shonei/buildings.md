buildings, or structures, are things that can be constructed in [shonei](/shonei/).

#### navigation structures 
- platform 
    - just a solid surface to place stuff on top of, or for mice to walk on
    - can be placed overlapping other structures
    - has several size variants 
- stairs
- ladder 
    - can be placed in the middle of a tile or hanging off a side surface 
    - faster than climbing bare cliffs or rope, slower than stairs or elevators
- rope 
    - like ladders, but require support from above instead of from below. a bit slower.
- rope bridge 
    - a way to cross gaps 
- stone bridge 
    - a pretty way to cross small gaps
- elevator 
    - a mechanically powered way for one mouse to quickly move up and down 
- train / rail / train station
    - a coal powered way for mice to quickly move long distances 

#### architecture 
- roof 
    - mostly aesthetic / grants satisfaction to mice
    - partially protects structures underneath from condition decay
    - has several wooden and clay tile variants
- bracket 
    - support hung off a side surface that can be built on. expands horizontal footprint 

#### other misc. structures
- torch
- lantern 
- bench 
    - for sitting, grants satisfaction
- fountain
    - decorative, grants satisfaction
- fireplace 
    - for warming up and chatting
- teahouse 
    - for drinking tea and chatting 
- clock 
    - displays exact day in UI
- thermometer 
    - displays precise temperature in UI
- work flag 
    - for setting mice's work anchors so that they work far from where they naturally would be

#### housing 
- shack 
    - cheap home, houses 1 mouse
- burrow
    - houses 2 mice, dug into solid ground
- house
    - a two story building that houses 3 mice

#### storage 
- crate 
    - stores one stack of 100 tails 
- drawer
    - stores four stacks of 20 tails 
- granary 
    - stores one stack of 1000 tails, can only hold wheat / rice / soybeans
- bin
    - holds one stack of 100 tails, but is open and lets items inside decay quickly
- tank 
    - stores one stack of 100 tails of a liquid 
- bookshelf 
    - stores up to 10 books

#### production 
- sawmill
    - woodworker makes planks from wood
- workshop
    - woodworker makes tools, furniture 
- digging pit 
    - digger digs for dirt, clay, or sand
- quarry
    - miner mines for stone and ores
- well 
    - hauler draws water from groundwater 
- pump
    - hauler draws water from pools / lakes
- plant pot 
    - farmer can grow plants, can be placed on any solid surface 
- greenhouse
    - a temperature insulated plant pot, for farmer to grow plants 
- mill 
    - cook grinds wheat or soybeans. faster if powered 
- steamer 
    - cook steams wheat into bread
- press 
    - cook presses soymilk into tofu
- brewery 
    - cook crafts yeast, or brews rice into wine
- weaver
    - clothier weaves fiber into cloth
- tailor 
    - clothier sews cloth into clothing
- hattery 
    - clothier makes hats out of fiber, bamboo, or cloth
- paper mill 
    - woodworker makes paper out of wood
- scriptorium 
    - scribe writes books
- laboratory 
    - scientists conduct research
- kiln
    - digger fires clay into molds, pots, tiles
- foundry 
    - metalworker melts ores, casts metal objects 
- bellows 
    - raises foundry maximum temperature for iron casting, requires power 
- forge 
    - metalworker forges cast iron into steel
- cauldron 
    - apothecary brews herbal tonics 
- alembic 
    - apothecary distills complex elixirs
- dyehouse
    - dyer grinds plants, charcoal, and soymilk into dye

#### mechanics 
- wheel 
    - runner generates mechanical power 
- windmill 
- flywheel 
    - store mechanical power
- power shaft 

#### monuments 
- monuments are required for advancing town tier 
- shrine 
    - grants small chance for slightly larger harvests
    - requirement to become village
- market gate 
    - enables merchants / trading, and is the portal through which merchants travel to the market 
    - requirement to become town
