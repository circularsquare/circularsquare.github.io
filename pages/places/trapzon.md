trapzon refers to a plateau and the highest mountain within that plateau. trapzon is located south of [shilla](/places/shilla/). trapzon is inhabited mostly by goat people who practice [magic](/anemos/magic/) and magical manufacturing. thereis little vegetation on the high stormy plateau, as winters and magically induced cold spells are frequent.

the mountain of trapzon has a different topology to normal mountains due to a high concentration of [trancin](/thoughts/trancin/). being the highest point in the known world, the flow of trancin focuses here and pulls the mountain up to an unknown height.
