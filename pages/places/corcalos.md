corcalos is an avian city-state perched on a mountain top overlooking the corcalos strait. it is a cosmopolitan hub for trade. 

the lower city is a port, with a diverse population. sea routes between [nachria](/places/nachria/) and [melissa](/places/melissa/) run through these waters. as the tide rises, whirlpools form on the east mouth of the strait, so travel is highly constrained. the newly constructed bypass canal locks are usually crowded as well.
