cooking is the preparation of [food](/thoughts/food/) with [heat](/thoughts/temperature/). cooking is a service. in most cases, if you cook for someone, you are doing them a favor.

cooking was an original source of conflict between me and [pine](/friends/pine/). i liked to cook, because it was new to me, and because i wanted people to [try](/thoughts/beingseen/) my food. i liked the idea of making a huge pot of curry and everyone being happy. i think because that's how it is in games. but i made the huge pot of curry, and then i just had a huge pot of curry, and pine didn't really want any. why didn't they just say so? i was sad. i was mad. 

cooking is sometimes the opposite of a favor. when you cook, if you do not handle it well, you place a burden on the eater to eat. i don't know how i didn't recognize this, given how often i felt burdened by the cooking of [aloe](/friends/aloe/) and [honeysuckle](/friends/honeysuckle/). i guess i forgot this when i grew into a teenager and become radically [individual](/thoughts/community/)ized.

cooking is a complicated net of feelings.
