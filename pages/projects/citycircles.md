hi!

[this game](/circles/) is heavily inspired by the quizzes at [hugequiz](https://hugequiz.com/) and [JetPunk](https://www.jetpunk.com/).

the city data is from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/). the cities in this database are not disjoint! for instance, both new york city and manhattan are in this data.

the population count is everyone who lives inside your circles, cities or not, from the [GHSL 2025 population grid](https://human-settlement.emergency.copernicus.eu/ghs_pop.php) at about 25 km. each grid square is cut into 64 small ones, and a small one is in if its middle is inside a circle, so edges are a bit rough. in a country or continent game it only counts people who live there, using borders from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/).

country, continent and band games use every city of 10k and up. europe and asia split at the urals, and oceania starts at the papua new guinea border.

this project was co-authored by claude.
